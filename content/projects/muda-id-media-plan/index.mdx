## Overview

Developed a comprehensive Q4 2026 media and performance strategy for Muda.Id, a modest fashion brand targeting career women and young mothers aged 23–38. The brief was to build a pitching-ready media plan that could take the brand from IDR 2.28B total GMV (Q3 baseline) to IDR 5.3B by December 2026 — with a Blended ROAS target of 25x.

The deliverable covered everything from performance diagnosis, product portfolio strategy, promotion mechanics, and full-funnel ad setup to a month-by-month media plan breakdown for October, November, and December.

## The Problem

Muda.Id had a strong product lineup and decent Shopee presence but was hitting a growth ceiling. Three root causes were identified:

1. **Low AOV** — The brand was selling single items rather than bundles, keeping basket size small
2. **High organic leakage** — Overreliance on paid ads with no affiliate or organic content engine
3. **Low live conversion (1.1%)** — Live sessions were using repetitive hard-sell formats that weren't converting

The gap between Q3 performance and Q4 targets was significant: Shopee needed to nearly double from IDR 2.1B to IDR 4.8B, while TikTok Store needed to scale from IDR 180M to IDR 500M.

## My Approach

Rather than treating each channel in isolation, I built an integrated strategy where each lever reinforces the others:

- **Product architecture** drives AOV through bundling
- **Affiliate network** (2,500 nano/micro affiliates) reduces paid ad dependency
- **Live streaming format shift** — from hard-sell to "Wardrobe Transformation & Mix-and-Match" format to lift conversion
- **Full-funnel ad structure** — upper funnel for awareness, GMV Max for conversion retargeting

The strategy was anchored to a Blended ROAS formula: (Total Organic GMV + Total Paid GMV) / Total Ads Spend across all platforms.

## Product Strategy

Four products were given distinct roles in the portfolio:

- **Ivana Skirt** — Volume & Conversion Driver (ironless, versatile bottoms)
- **Riva Blouse** — Basket Size Inflator (daily career wear)
- **Signature Dress** — GMV & Gross Margin Escalator (hero item, highest margin)
- **Scarf Printing** — First-time Buyer Hook & Add-on Cart (triggers free shipping threshold)

Two bundles were created to push AOV: **Career Mom 2-in-1 Bundle** (Ivana Skirt + Riva Blouse) at Rp 369,000, and **Executive Elegance Set** (Signature Dress + Scarf Printing) at Rp 499,000.

## Channel Strategy

### Shopee Ads (GMV Max Strategy)
Structured around Store Ads (target ROAS 5–6.5x) and Product Ads (target ROAS 8–12x), using keyword targeting around "basic hijab", "baju wanita muslimah", "rok/skirt modest", and "tunik elegan".

### TikTok Ads (Full-Funnel)
Four-priority structure:
1. **Upper Funnel** — Spark Ads targeting women 23–38 in major cities, Video Views objective, 3–5 creatives in 15–30 sec lifestyle formats
2. **KOL Proposals** — 1 Macro KOL (Indah Nada Puspita) + 3 Micro KOLs for trust and engagement
3. **Affiliate Strategy** — 50 affiliates (10 Micro, 40 Nano), 10% base commission + performance bonus
4. **GMV Max Ads** — Retargeting using top-performing Spark Ads creatives

### Content Strategy
Three short-form video pillars:
- 40% Pain Point Solutions ("baju kerja tanpa setrika")
- 30% Premium Aesthetic Lookbook
- 30% UGC/KOC Review

Five content briefs were created covering soft-sell organic content ("Drama Pagi Cari Baju"), lifestyle vlogs, and hard-sell promo formats with urgency mechanics.

## Monthly Media Plan

### October 2026 — Foundation & Testing
- Shopee GMV target: IDR 2.4B (~85%), TikTok: IDR 200M (~15%)
- Total spend: Rp 105M (Shopee Rp 85M + TikTok Rp 20M)
- Key activity: **10.10 Early Career Refresh** — 10% discount + Free Scarf Pouch, Affiliate Booster 10.10 with 3% extra commission

### November 2026 — Scale-Up & Bundling
- Shopee GMV target: IDR 3.6B (~80%), TikTok: IDR 350M (~20%)
- Total spend: Rp 160M (Shopee Rp 130M + TikTok Rp 30M)
- Key activity: **11.11 Bundle Craze** — Smart Mom Pack (Ivana Skirt + Riva Blouse + GRATIS Scarf Printing), Shopee Live VIP Pass with Rp 40k voucher every hour

### December 2026 — Peak Harvest & Max ROAS
- Shopee GMV target: IDR 4.8B (~75%), TikTok: IDR 500M (~25%)
- Total spend: Rp 200M (~3.77% of GMV), Shopee Rp 160M + TikTok Rp 40M
- Key activities: **12.12 Year-End Glamour** Flash Sale (Signature Dress Rp 329k, 00:00–02:00 WIB), Payday Special Mother's Reward Set, TikTok Live Non-Stop Marathon 12.12 (24-hour live with Spin the Wheel mechanic)

## Performance Control System

A weekly audit matrix was established across all platforms:
- Meta Ads — ROAS target ≥ 10x
- TikTok Ads — ROAS target ≥ 15x
- Shopee Ads — ROAS target ≥ 10x
- Affiliate Content — GMV contribution tracking
- Shopee Live — Voucher Claim Rate ≥ 5%

If Paid ROAS drops below 15x, the response protocol is: increase affiliate content traffic, raise Shopee Live voucher claim rate, amplify top-performing creatives, optimize targeting.

## Competitor Audit

A side-by-side audit of Muda.Id vs. Klamby (direct competitor) revealed:

**Shopee:** Muda.Id was missing Double Date Promo, Flash Sale, and Voucher Toko features that Klamby had active — gaps that directly impact conversion.

**TikTok content:** Klamby's relational POV hooks ("Jealousy in friendship?") achieved 28.3K views vs. Muda.Id's influencer-led promo hooks at 8.9K views. Key insight: relatable situational hooks outperform celebrity-led promo content for this audience.

## What I Learned

Building a media plan at this level of specificity — down to named promotion mechanics, hourly live session schedules, and per-channel ROAS targets — revealed how much strategic clarity depends on understanding the unit economics first.

The most important decision in the plan wasn't which channels to use, but how to structure the product portfolio to make every channel more effective. Without the bundling mechanic, hitting the AOV target would have been impossible at the planned ad spend level.

I also learned that competitor audits are most useful when they reveal feature gaps, not just creative differences. The Shopee audit found concrete, activatable gaps (missing Flash Sale and Voucher Toko) rather than vague "they're doing better" observations.
