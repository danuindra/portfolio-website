## Positioning

**Siapa Danu di mata audiens target?**

> Danu Indra Ismawan adalah seorang Digital Marketer & Analyst yang tidak hanya belajar teori —
> tetapi membuktikan kemampuannya melalui project nyata, data, dan hasil yang terukur.

> **Catatan:** Kata "Aspiring" dihapus secara intentional. Self-qualifying titles melemahkan positioning sebelum rekruter membaca lebih jauh. Lihat `24-content-review.md` Issue 1.

**Positioning Statement:**

> "An entry-level digital marketer who combines analytical thinking with creative execution,
> backed by real-world projects in marketing analytics, AI-assisted productivity, and data-driven campaigns."

**Diferensiasi dari kandidat lain:**
- Tidak hanya belajar — langsung membangun dan mendokumentasikan project nyata
- Menggabungkan kemampuan marketing dengan pemahaman teknologi (AI, analytics, web)
- Pendekatan terstruktur dan product-thinking dalam setiap pekerjaan

## Unique Value Proposition

**Apa yang hanya Danu bisa tawarkan?**

> I bridge the gap between marketing strategy and technical execution —
> combining data analytics, AI tools, and digital marketing fundamentals
> to deliver work that is measurable, scalable, and built to last.

**Tiga pilar keunikan:**

| Pilar | Deskripsi |
|---|---|
| **Analytical** | Bekerja dengan data — GA4, Looker Studio, dashboard reporting |
| **Technical** | Memahami web, AI tools, dan cara kerja platform digital |
| **Strategic** | Berpikir jangka panjang, bukan sekadar eksekusi tugas |

## Tone of Voice

**Cara Danu berkomunikasi di website, caption, dan tulisan:**

### Prinsip Utama
- **Clear over clever** — Mudah dipahami lebih penting dari terkesan pintar
- **Confident but humble** — Percaya diri tanpa arogan, jujur tentang level saat ini
- **Direct** — Langsung ke inti, tidak bertele-tele
- **Human** — Tidak seperti press release perusahaan — tetap personal dan nyata

### Contoh Tone

| ❌ Hindari | ✅ Gunakan |
|---|---|
| "Passionate professional with extensive expertise..." | "I'm learning digital marketing by building real projects." |
| "Synergizing data-driven solutions..." | "I use GA4 and Looker Studio to turn data into decisions." |
| "Results-oriented team player..." | "Here's what I built, and here's what I learned from it." |

## Personal Story

**Narasi singkat yang menjelaskan perjalanan Danu:**

> I didn't start with a clear career path in digital marketing.
> But I've always been drawn to understanding how things work —
> how people make decisions, how platforms grow, and how data tells stories.
>
> I approach marketing work analytically — building systems to measure
> what's working, document what isn't, and improve over time.
> Every project is a real test of that approach, not just a learning exercise.
>
> This portfolio documents the work: what was built, why it was built that way,
> and what came out of it. That's the most honest way to show what I can do.
